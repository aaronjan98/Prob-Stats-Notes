3. Just in case professor doesn't include a z-table and we're asked a normal distribution problem, then you must know the Empirical Rule: $X \sim N(\mu, \sigma^{2})$
	- area between $\mu- \sigma$ and $\mu+ \sigma$ is $68.27\%$
	- area between $\mu - 2\sigma$ and $\mu+2\sigma$ is $95.45\%$
	- area between $\mu - 3\sigma$ and $\mu+3\sigma$ is $99.73\%$
	- **Example 1.** $X \sim N(3, 4^2)$
		- Find $P(X < 7) = \Phi(1)$
	- **Example 2.** $P(\lvert X-3 \rvert < 8)$
		- **N.B.**:  this problem is within two standard deviation, but if you're not sure, you can standardize...
		- $= P(-8 < X - 3 < 8)$
		- $= P(-8 + 3 < X < 8+3)$
		- $=P(-5 < X < 11)$
		- then divide by 4 to get $95.45\%$
